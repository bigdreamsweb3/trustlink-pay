TSN is hybrid Internet of Value settlement infrastructure: off-chain services coordinate identity, payment authorization, and verification, while on-chain programs enforce settlement rules on Solana. TIP manages TIN identity and route resolution. The TSN SDK helps applications work with configured services, prepare wallet-authorized requests, and follow their status. Cross-network interoperability remains route-specific and must be verified before being treated as available.

GPRU is designed to guard the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to TCAP. Its hidden-state proof should show that the state is current and that a proposed balance change follows from it. TCAP governs approved assets, reserves, and liquidity, then applies valid authorized changes atomically.

## Availability

The [November 16 Devnet record](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/blob/main/docs/laboratory/experiments/EXP-20261116-private-credit-epoch.md) establishes two confirmed native private credits and complete accounting of their shared epoch. Confidential debit/exit, owner-history completeness and mixed-action epoch accounting require separate evidence. Verify the selected network and relevant transaction result before treating an application flow as available.

Experimental native credit uses a private-secret nullifier and fresh blinded amount commitment, with no individual reserve-liability change. TCAP owns complete 2–64-credit epoch accounting and publishes the total after GPRU proof verification. The [TCAP lifecycle](/layers/tcap#native-credit-epoch-lifecycle) explains Node scheduling, shared account creation and reuse. Trusted Node access to routes/openings and public wallet funding are accepted boundaries. Public roots, points, accounts, ciphertext, timing and known registry/head history can still correlate TCAP actions; confirmed execution does not establish public-observer unlinkability. See [the privacy limits](/security/threat-model).
