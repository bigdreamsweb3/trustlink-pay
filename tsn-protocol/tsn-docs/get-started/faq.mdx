## What is TSN?

TSN (Transfer Settlement Network) is the full settlement infrastructure. TSNP is its on-chain Transfer Settlement Network Protocol. TIP, GPRU, and TCAP are distinct protocol layers.

## Is TIP the same as TIN?

No. TIP is the Transfer Identity Protocol. A TIN is one 10-digit Transfer Identity Number managed within TIP.

## What does GPRU guard?

GPRU guards the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to TCAP for a balance-changing action. Its proof is intended to establish that the hidden state is current and that the proposed transition follows from it. TCAP governs the funds and applies valid authorized changes atomically.

## Does a GPRU proof move funds?

No. A proof is one check in the settlement process. It does not grant custody or replace sender authorization, accepted-asset checks, or TCAP's reserve and liquidity rules.

## Is the hidden-head proof flow available end to end?

It is not established as an end-to-end settlement capability. Confidential debit and exit are also not established as available. Check current capability and network status before relying on a feature.

## How does privacy differ between TIN and wallet transfers?

The current native-credit ABI does not encode a plaintext recipient TIN or private predecessor pointer. It uses shared TIP/TCAP roots, and the owner retains private record lineage. Public action fields, encrypted Memo bytes, accounts, signatures, ordering and funding wallets remain observable. That encoding boundary does not establish that metadata or authorized service/application knowledge cannot correlate payments. The complete hidden-head flow and confidential debit/exit remain separate unverified runtime capabilities; see the [security boundary](/security/threat-model).

## When is a payment complete?

A request being accepted, verified, or submitted is not the same as settlement. A payment is confirmed only after the relevant Solana transaction succeeds. If funding and credit use separate transactions, check both stages.
