## What TSN Is

TSN is a **Decentralized Settlement Protocol (DESP)** implementation designed to fix fragmentation in blockchain payments. It separates identity, authorization, liquidity, and settlement, similar to how traditional financial networks separate clearing and settlement.

**Key characteristics:**

- Identity-first: Uses a portable Transfer Identity Number (TIN) instead of wallet addresses.
- Privacy-preserving: Balances are stored as encrypted TCAP commitments rather than public amounts.
- Intent-based: Payments are described and authorized off-chain, then settled on-chain.
- Cross-chain: Solana is the source domain; Creditcoin/EVM routes handle destination settlement.
