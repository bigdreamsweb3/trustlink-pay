---
title: "Community and Governance"
description: "How TSN separates community input from user authorization and on-chain settlement authority."
---

TrustLink Labs welcomes users, protocol engineers, cryptographers, security researchers, and open-source contributors to help review TSN and shape its development.

Community input can inform protocol priorities and future network participation. It does not authorize an individual user's payment, create token liquidity, or replace the on-chain rules enforced by TSNP and TCAP. The sender's wallet authorizes payment; GPRU is intended to guard the private state presented to TCAP; TCAP governs assets and applies valid transitions atomically.

Formal route governance and permissionless operator participation should not be treated as active capabilities unless their proposal, voting, and activation rules are published and verified. Current engineering and network-operation details belong in the [repository documentation](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/tree/main/docs).

Contributors can begin at [TrustLink Labs on GitHub](https://github.com/Trustlink-Labs) or the [TrustLink Pay protocol repository](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay). The [contribution guide](/about/contribute) links to research and SDK material.
