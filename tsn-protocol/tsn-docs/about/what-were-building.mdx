TrustLink Labs is building **TSN**, hybrid infrastructure organized around a decentralized settlement protocol for the **Internet of Value**. TSN combines off-chain coordination and verification with on-chain authorization and asset settlement. [Solana](/overview/solana-integration) is the current experimental implementation; [Base, Arbitrum and Polygon](/overview/cross-network-roadmap) are planned lower-fee destinations whose routes need implementation and verification before availability is announced.

The network combines protocol layers with application SDKs so a payment can be addressed through a portable identity, authorized by a wallet, checked against private state, and settled under asset-specific rules.

**TSN** names the full infrastructure. **TSNP** is the on-chain Transfer Settlement Network Protocol. **TIP** manages transfer identity, including the 10-digit **TIN**. **GPRU** guards the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to **TCAP**. TCAP governs the accepted assets, reserves, and settlement transition. **DIP** describes the deposit-ingress protocol for external value. Circle's **CCTP** is a separate protocol used only where an integration supports it.

The design goal is to keep identity, private-state verification, and asset governance in distinct roles. GPRU's proof is intended to show that a hidden state is current and that a proposed change follows from it; TCAP remains the authority over funds and applies authorized changes atomically.

Native private credit and complete epoch accounting have bounded Devnet execution evidence. Confidential debit/exit, mixed-action epoch accounting, owner-history completeness and public-observer action unlinkability require separate verification. Trusted Node verification and public wallet funding are accepted boundaries. For current implementation and deployment status, see [Current Protocol Capabilities](/about/current-technology). The detailed engineering record is maintained in the [repository documentation](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/tree/main/docs).
