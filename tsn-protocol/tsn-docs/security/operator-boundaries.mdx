sidebarTitle: "Service Boundaries"
description: "The limits of application, verification-service, transaction-submission, proof, and settlement authority."
---

TSN services coordinate payment work, but the settlement programs enforce the on-chain rules. The sender authorizes the payment, services verify and route the request, and a Cranker submits the authorized transaction. Only successful program execution on Solana confirms the corresponding state change.

GPRU guards the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to TCAP. TCAP governs assets, reserves, and liquidity, then applies an authorized transition atomically. A service response or a GPRU proof alone cannot move funds.

The native-credit proof hides the TIN route and predecessor witness from the on-chain statement, but its amount, asset, shared roots, nullifier, successor point and encrypted Memo remain public. The published nullifier is derived from public deposit data, so funding and credit can be linked. The Node resolves private routing and constructs the witness, so this boundary does not establish owner-only knowledge or immunity to timing and transaction correlation. Direct wallet transfers expose their endpoints; confidential debit and exit require separate execution evidence. See [the privacy limits](/security/threat-model) for the current encoding and trust assumptions.
