Transfer Settlement Network is hybrid settlement infrastructure for the Internet of Value. Its cross-network direction is to route authorized value using a TIN while enforcing the source and destination's asset, authorization and settlement requirements. The current native implementation and bounded execution evidence are on Solana Devnet; this roadmap does not announce an operational EVM payout route.

## Planned lower-fee destinations

The current integration priorities are **Base, Arbitrum and Polygon**, subject to route design, asset support, implementation and verification. Base and Arbitrum are Ethereum Layer 2 networks; Polygon PoS is an EVM-compatible sidechain. Their fees differ by network and transaction conditions, so lower fees do not mean fee-free settlement. Ethereum mainnet is outside the present integration scope because of its fee costs; this does not exclude networks that use Ethereum for their own settlement or security model.

Naming these priorities selects a development direction rather than deploying contracts, enabling liquidity or proving a cross-network payment. No network's compatibility with EVM alone establishes that TSN can settle an asset there. The [SDK integration responsibilities](/sdk/integrations) explain the distinction between an exported helper and an available service.

## Requirements for an available route

A supported route needs an explicit source action, destination network and executor, accepted token, authorized liquidity, replay protection and a defined confirmation policy. The sender must approve the exact destination and payment conditions. Node verification must match those conditions; Cranker execution cannot replace them with another route.

Source debit or commitment publication and destination payout are distinct observations. Route configuration, a submitted transaction or a success status from a service cannot establish final delivery. Before a route is presented as available, its contracts and service integration must be reviewed and source/destination receipts retained for the intended asset and environment. Confidential debit/exit and mixed-action accounting retain their own unresolved checks.
