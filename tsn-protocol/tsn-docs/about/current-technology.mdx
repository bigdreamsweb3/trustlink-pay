TSN is hybrid Internet of Value settlement infrastructure: off-chain services coordinate identity, payment authorization, and verification, while on-chain programs enforce settlement rules on Solana. TIP manages TIN identity and route resolution. The TSN SDK helps applications work with configured services, prepare wallet-authorized requests, and follow their status. Cross-network interoperability remains route-specific and must be verified before being treated as available.

GPRU is designed to guard the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to TCAP. Its hidden-state proof should show that the state is current and that a proposed balance change follows from it. TCAP governs approved assets, reserves, and liquidity, then applies valid authorized changes atomically.

## Availability

The hidden-head proof flow is not established as an end-to-end settlement capability. Confidential debit and exit are not available as verified end-to-end features. Do not infer availability from a source file or an SDK helper; verify the selected network and the relevant transaction result.

The documentation does not claim mainnet readiness, a completed independent cryptographic audit, or zero transaction linkability. Public-chain metadata remains visible. Detailed build, deployment, proof, and service status belongs in the [repository documentation](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/tree/main/docs).
