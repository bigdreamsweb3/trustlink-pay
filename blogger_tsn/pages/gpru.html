GPRU: Proof Verification for TSN Private Credit

The Guarded Privacy Routing Unit verifies hidden route, balance, nullifier and Memo relations for TSN. TCAP retains custody and accounting responsibility.

GPRU's responsibility

GPRU is a standalone on-chain verifier within TSN's hybrid settlement infrastructure. In the current experimental Solana credit flow, it verifies a Groth16 proof that binds authenticated recipient route membership, the current private balance head, exact funded amount, the secret-derived spend nullifier and the successor balance. It does not hold TCAP reserve tokens or act as the off-chain intent service.

Authorization and proof order

The owner authorizes the intent and exact reserve deposit. Receiver coordinates work and leases; Node verifies funding and constructs the private witness and owner Memo. Mother authorizes the inspected TSNP settlement. Cranker submits the permitted TSNP call, TCAP invokes GPRU, and proof success allows TCAP to advance the shared state and credit-epoch transcript atomically.

The public statement

The current circuit has thirteen public inputs, including shared roots, Memo and action commitments, a nullifier, TIP route root, successor portfolio and balance points, encrypted blinding delta, ephemeral points and authenticated funding root. The individual credit amount is private. GPRU binds the following Memo's bytes and prefix fields to that proof; an encrypted record alone is not proof of correct arithmetic.

Configuration and privacy limits

The running circuit, proving key, verifier key and initialized account must match. Wallet deposits, token identifiers, roots, nullifiers, ciphertext, accounts, timing and aggregate totals remain observable. Node handles private routes and openings. Independent cryptographic review, general unlinkability and confidential spending remain separate requirements.
