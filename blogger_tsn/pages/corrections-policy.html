Corrections Policy

Trustworthy technical writing depends on making errors visible and correcting them carefully.

How to report an issue

Email support@trustlinklabs.io with the page URL, the exact passage, a description of the issue, and supporting evidence or a primary source.

How corrections are handled

We review reports for accuracy, relevance, and source quality. Confirmed factual errors may be corrected in place. Material changes to a technical claim or interpretation may receive an explanatory note or update date.

Scope of corrections

This policy covers factual, attribution, link, and technical-status errors. Differences of opinion about an explicitly labeled proposal or research interpretation are not necessarily corrections, but useful evidence may still be incorporated into a future revision.

Editorial standard

The publication distinguishes external evidence, TrustLink Labs architecture, and implementation status. Corrections preserve that distinction and do not represent the protocol as regulated or production-ready unless the evidence supports that description.
