# TCAP: Transfer Confidential Asset Protocol

TCAP governs approved assets, reserve vaults, and protocol liability accounting. In the current TSN credit path, TCAP applies an authorized commitment transition only after TSN validates the GPRU DNA key proof and invokes TCAP through its approved CPI path.

## Current credit behavior

The Node resolves the recipient's TIP route and constructs the GPRU-scoped credit authorization and DBrix Memo record. TSN verifies the authorization digest and asks GPRU to validate the transaction-specific key proof and encrypted balance transition. TCAP then checks the asset, policy, validity window, predecessor commitment, and next sequence. The successful instruction advances the replay head atomically.

The funding-derived commitment is carried in the transition's `nullifier` field and bound into the authorization digest. The same TCAP state update records the latest nullifier, successor commitment, and sequence. This rejects replay of the same settlement without a separate spent-funding record or per-transaction PDA.

The encrypted balance ciphertext and encrypted transaction history are carried in the transaction's DBrix Memo instruction. The TCAP replay-head account stores only the latest commitment, sequence, policy commitment, nullifier, and frozen flag; it does not hold a ciphertext or history. Credit updates use homomorphic ElGamal addition, while debit and exit remain fail-closed until TCAP can verify balance sufficiency and the exact subtraction.

## Credit and debit requirements

For a debit, a proof system must establish that the prior encrypted balance covers the amount and that the successor reflects the exact subtraction. For a TIN-to-TIN transfer, proofs must establish conservation between source and destination while binding both state transitions to the same governed asset. TCAP must apply the resulting reserve-liability changes atomically after the proofs pass.

Wallet deposits and exits expose the public wallet and token-transfer metadata required at that boundary. GPRU's one-time DNA output reduces reuse of a stable recipient key; it does not conceal public funding metadata or prove balance correctness by itself.

## Source boundaries

- TIP resolves TIN identity and publishes the route material used by GPRU.
- GPRU derives and verifies transaction-specific one-time key material.
- TSN validates the settlement authorization and coordinates the GPRU-to-TCAP CPI path.
- TCAP enforces asset policy and atomically advances its commitment state.

The program instruction and account layouts remain the source of truth for deployed behavior. See [GPRU confidential state](/architecture/gpru-confidential-balances) for the memo history format and debit proof boundary.
