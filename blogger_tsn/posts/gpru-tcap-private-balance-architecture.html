Transfer Confidential Asset Protocol: The Authorization and Private Balance Architecture of TSN



Modern payment networks need two protections at once: a verifiable authorization path and a private balance path. TrustLink Labs separates those responsibilities across GPRU, TSN, and TCAP.

Current architecture checkpoint

This August research article was reconciled on November 22 with the current experimental private-credit contract. Earlier per-TIN tip, Settlement DNA and Epoch Treasury descriptions belong to a distinct retained family; they do not describe native shared-root credit.

Applications can address digital value through a ten-digit Transfer Identity Number (TIN). Transfer Settlement Network (TSN) is hybrid decentralized settlement protocol infrastructure for the Internet of Value, combining off-chain verification and work coordination with on-chain authorization, private-credit proof verification and reserve accounting. Solana is the current experimental domain.

Identity and exact approval

TIP manages registration, authenticated recipient-route membership and owner recovery. The sender signs a TIN-targeted intent; Node resolves the permitted route and validates current state. The owner separately approves the exact TCAP wallet deposit. A TIN is not a private key, public balance account or proof of legal identity.

Verification and confidential accounting

Receiver coordinates restricted work and leases; Node verifies confirmed funding and constructs the private witness and encrypted owner Memo. Mother authorizes TSNP, Cranker submits the permitted transaction, and TCAP invokes GPRU before advancing the shared head/nullifier root and blinded credit transcript. TCAP owns reserve custody; GPRU verifies proof relations without holding reserve tokens. Complete aggregate accounting is separate from individual credit.

Evidence and future integration

The retained November 16 Solana Devnet checkpoint verifies two private credits and complete aggregate accounting. Wallet funding, roots, token, ciphertext, accounts, timing and aggregate totals remain public; Node handles sensitive routes and openings. General unlinkability, owner recovery, confidential spending and cross-network delivery require separate evidence. Base, Arbitrum and Polygon PoS are planned lower-fee targets with distinct security assumptions; they have transaction fees. Optional verified TIN credentials are proposed work.

Private state and owner recovery

Current private credit proves exact funded-note membership and amount, consumes a secret-derived nullifier and binds the encrypted owner Memo to the successor balance. Its thirteen-input statement authenticates current shared state and TIP route membership. Owner-held scan material can recover the proof-bound blinding delta; complete history recovery must validate openings, authorization, sequence and predecessor linkage against settled transactions.

Separate aggregate accounting

Individual credit advances shared state and the blinded asset-epoch transcript without moving reserve tokens or reclassifying public per-credit liabilities. A separate proof covers the complete ordered set of 2–64 accepted credits before TCAP reclassifies their total. One confirmed deposit can support an individual credit; the batch minimum applies to accounting.
