# Investment Opportunity: TrustLink Pay TSN

TrustLink Pay is building identity-first, privacy-preserving payments on Solana. The Transfer Settlement Network (TSN) creates a new category of yield opportunity for stablecoin issuers, liquidity providers, and operators.

**This document is for**: Investors, stablecoin issuers, liquidity providers, and strategic partners.

## The Problem We Solve

### Crypto Payments Have a UX Crisis

| Issue | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Wallet addresses | 26+ characters to type or paste — error-prone and frustrating |
| Address poisoning | Hackers send tiny amounts to guess your address, leading to scams |
| Privacy leakage | Every transaction links your wallet to the recipient's |
| Centralization | Only large exchanges can provide phone-based payments (Venmo, OPay, UPI) |

### The Solution: Identity-First Payments

TrustLink Pay lets users send stablecoins to phone numbers — as familiar as mobile money.

```
Sender: "Send 100 USDC to +234 801 234 5678"
TrustLink: "Confirm: Sending 100 USDC to Daniel (Verified)"
Sender: "Confirm" ✓
```

No wallet addresses to paste. No privacy leakage. Payment happens in minutes, not days.

## The TSN Opportunity

### What is TSN?

The Transfer Settlement Network is a privacy-preserving settlement layer that:
- Enables instant, private payments to phone numbers
- Protects sender and recipient wallet addresses
- Distributes settlement fees to liquidity providers and operators

### Why TSN Creates Yield

TSN creates value through settlement fees. Every payment generates revenue that flows to participants:

```
Payment Volume → Settlement Fees → 87% LPs / 5% Operators / 8% Treasury
```

**This is real yield from real economic activity — not token emissions.**

## For Stablecoin Issuers

### The Opportunity

Your stablecoin is used for daily payments — not just trading and DeFi.

| Without TSN | With TSN |
|-------------|----------|
| Users hold your stablecoin for speculation | Users spend your stablecoin for payments |
| Volume tied to market conditions | Volume driven by real-world usage |
| Limited use cases | Payment rail for millions of users |
| No direct relationship with users | Direct engagement through TrustLink |

### How to Participate

**Option 1: Fund a Vault**

Provide liquidity for your token in the TSN:

1. TrustLink deploys a vault for your token
2. You fund the vault with your stablecoin
3. LPs can also deposit, or you can be the sole LP
4. Payments using your token generate fees
5. You earn 87% of settlement fees from your token's volume

**Benefits:**
- Earn yield on your stablecoin reserves
- Drive adoption as your token becomes the preferred settlement option
- No operational overhead — just fund and earn

**Option 2: Run a Cranker**

Deploy and operate your own cranker node:

1. Run the tsn-cranker-op-daemon on your infrastructure
2. Fund a vault for your token (or use existing vault)
3. Execute payments and earn operator fees (5%)
4. Promote your token to users

**Benefits:**
- Full control over settlement for your token
- Additional earnings from operator share
- First-mover advantage in your token's settlement network
- Partner with TrustLink for infrastructure or run independently

### Case Study: USDC at Launch

TrustLink Pay will deploy the first USDC vault at launch and operate the primary cranker.

**Why USDC issuers should participate:**
- $150B+ market cap with limited real-world payment use cases
- TSN creates a payment rail for USDC

## FAQ for Investors

### What is the minimum investment?

For vault funding, minimum is $10,000 USDC. For strategic partnerships, contact us.

### Is there a lock-up period?

Deposits can be withdrawn at epoch boundaries (approximately every 7 hours). No long-term lock-up required.

### What happens if volume drops?

Yields scale with volume. Lower volume = lower yields, but no negative yields (capital remains stable for stablecoin vaults).

### What are the risks?

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Smart contract | Audited code, but always some residual risk |
| Vault utilization | Monitor and adjust position |
| Token depeg | Use established stablecoins |
| Protocol changes | Transparent governance |

### How do I track my position?

TrustLink provides a dashboard for vault monitoring, yield tracking, and position management.

### Can I integrate with my existing systems?

Yes. TSN provides APIs for programmatic vault management, position tracking, and reporting.
