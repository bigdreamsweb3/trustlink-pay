## What is TSN?

TSN (Transfer Settlement Network) is hybrid Internet of Value infrastructure organized around a decentralized settlement protocol. TSNP is its on-chain Transfer Settlement Network Protocol; TIP, GPRU and TCAP are distinct protocol layers. Off-chain coordination and verification connect to on-chain settlement enforcement, with [current development on Solana](/overview/solana-integration).

## Is TIP the same as TIN?

No. TIP is the Transfer Identity Protocol. A TIN is one 10-digit Transfer Identity Number managed within TIP.

## What does GPRU guard?

GPRU guards the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to TCAP for a balance-changing action. Its proof is intended to establish that the hidden state is current and that the proposed transition follows from it. TCAP governs the funds and applies valid authorized changes atomically.

## Does a GPRU proof move funds?

No. A proof is one check in the settlement process. It does not grant custody or replace sender authorization, accepted-asset checks, or TCAP's reserve and liquidity rules.

## Is the hidden-head proof flow available end to end?

The [current capability record](/about/current-technology) links finalized Devnet evidence for two native private credits and complete accounting of their shared epoch. That bounded result does not establish confidential debit/exit, owner-history completeness or public-observer action unlinkability. Check the selected network and applicable transaction result before relying on a feature.

## How does privacy differ between TIN and wallet transfers?

The primary privacy target is to hide TIN participation from public observers and prevent linking TCAP actions, including intended TIN-to-TIN transfers. Trusted Node access to verification routes, amounts and openings is accepted, as is public wallet funding for the mobile-money flow. Native credit omits the plaintext recipient TIN and private predecessor pointer, but public fields, timing and registration history can still support action correlation. TIP registration publicly associates registries with registration owner keys; current-source dictionary and cross-asset controls remain conditional on known-genesis or maintained-history assumptions. The two latest real credits were not matched to owners. See [the security boundary](/security/threat-model).

## When is a payment complete?

A request being accepted, verified, or submitted is not the same as settlement. A payment is confirmed only after the relevant Solana transaction succeeds. If funding and credit use separate transactions, check both stages.

## App downloads and free trials

TSN is developing its experimental first release, and TSN XP is its reference application. This handbook does not announce an app-store download, production hosted payment service or free-trial offer. Developers can inspect [the SDK](/sdk/getting-started) and [current capabilities](/about/current-technology); an experimental application demonstration is not a production availability claim.

## Planned EVM networks and verified names

Base, Arbitrum and Polygon are planned lower-fee destinations, with Ethereum mainnet outside the current integration scope. Their naming does not announce an operational route or fee-free settlement; [the roadmap](/overview/cross-network-roadmap) explains the missing implementation and evidence. TIN display names are currently owner-provided, while [issuer-backed credentials](/overview/identity-attestations) remain a proposed extension.
