TrustLink Labs develops and tests TSN and its related identity and asset protocols on Solana Devnet. The sections below distinguish current source-backed settlement behavior from encrypted-balance capabilities that remain future work.

## What is on Devnet today

- **TSN payment intents** — Sender-signed intents binding amount, mint, fee limits, expiry, nonce, and recipient route commitment.
- **TIP and TIN registry** — TIP issues and resolves transfer identities; the registry stores 10-digit TIN records, route commitments, and encrypted identity envelopes.
- **GPRU key verification** — TSN's credit instruction invokes the GPRU program to verify the one-time ElGamal DNA key proof against TIP route material. This verifies key derivation, not encrypted balance arithmetic.
- **Mother Escrow authority** — Epoch coordination and `ConfidentialSettlement` authorization through the one-time `SettlementDna` PDA.
- **TSN Node verification** — Stateless signature verification, route commitment matching, nonce/replay checks, and short-lived permit signing.
- **Cranker submission** — Leased execution of the exact authorized transaction without mutation of bound fields.
- **Epoch Treasury** — Protocol-controlled accounting for aggregate funding and settlement liability; no per-payment escrow or vault.
- **TCAP current behavior** — Existing deployed instructions and account layouts define current behavior. Opaque tip commitment advancement is not itself proof of a spendable balance.
- **Confidential debit and exit** — Disabled and fail-closed pending sound on-chain proof verification.

## Explicitly out of scope

The following are separate milestones and are not presented as active production capabilities:

- Recurring payments and subscription-provider execution.
- Confidential debits and exits (proof-gated and disabled).
- Encrypted-balance proof verification and confidential debit or exit.
- Mainnet deployment.
- Formal cryptographic audits.
- Perfect unlinkability.
