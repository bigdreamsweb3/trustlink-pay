About the Transfer Settlement Network

Address digital value with a ten-digit payment identity. Transfer Settlement Network (TSN) is hybrid decentralized settlement protocol infrastructure for the Internet of Value, combining off-chain coordination with on-chain authorization, proof verification and reserve accounting.

About TrustLink Labs

TrustLink Labs is a research and protocol-development initiative led by Agbaka Matthew Daniel, also known as Big Dreams Web3. The work explores how blockchain systems can coordinate payment identity, authorization, settlement execution, confidentiality, and final ownership updates.

What this publication covers

This publication explains the TSN architecture and related components, including TIN payment identity, GPRU proof verification, the TSN Node, Crankers, TSNP authorization and TCAP confidential asset accounting. Articles distinguish documented implementation evidence from proposed architecture and future work.

Current and planned integration

The experimental Solana implementation focuses on wallet deposits to private TIN credit. Lower-fee EVM targets include Base, Arbitrum and Polygon PoS; Ethereum Layer 1 is outside the current priority. These networks have fees and distinct security assumptions. Optional issuer-verified personal or business credentials remain proposed work, requiring owner consent, validity and revocation checks, and selective disclosure.

Research status

TSN is protocol research and implementation work. It is not presented as a regulated payment rail, bank, custodian, investment product, or universal replacement for existing payment networks. Technical status and limitations are stated in the relevant specifications and repositories.
