TSN Solana Credit Flow: Wallet Deposit to TIN Credit

How hybrid TSN infrastructure coordinates a signed wallet deposit, private TIN credit and separate aggregate accounting on Solana.

Hybrid infrastructure and actor order

Transfer Settlement Network (TSN) combines off-chain intent verification and durable work coordination with on-chain authorization, proof verification and reserve accounting. TIP manages ten-digit TIN payment identities, TSNP authorizes settlement, TCAP governs assets and reserves, and GPRU verifies hidden route and balance transitions. This architecture supports the Internet of Value direction without replacing Solana consensus.

The current experimental sequence

  1. The sender reviews the recipient TIN, asset, amount, fee and expiry, then signs the canonical intent.
  2. Node verifies message-only authorization and Receiver records funding readiness. The owner separately approves the exact TCAP deposit transaction.
  3. Cranker submits the wallet-signed funding wire unchanged. TCAP verifies the token transfer and appends the asset-and-amount-bound funded leaf.
  4. After confirmed funding, Node reconstructs current TIP and TCAP witnesses, encrypts the owner Memo and proves the private amount transition.
  5. Mother authorizes the inspected settlement and Cranker submits the permitted TSNP call followed by its bound Memo. TSNP invokes TCAP, which invokes GPRU.
  6. Proof success advances the shared head/nullifier root and blinded asset-epoch transcript atomically. Separate complete aggregate accounting reclassifies the accepted total.

Funding and accounting are separate

A single genuine confirmed deposit can support an individual credit without a funding pool or a source-count wait. Individual credit transfers no reserve tokens and performs no public per-credit liability reclassification. The separate aggregate proof covers 2–64 accepted credits. TCAP's credit epoch differs from the retained TSNP Epoch Treasury, AcceptedIntent, payout and refund family.

Privacy and authority

Wallet funding amounts and endpoints remain public, while the credit amount is privately committed. Token identifiers, roots, nullifiers, encrypted Memo bytes, accounts, timing and aggregate totals remain observable. Node intentionally handles sensitive routes and openings. Receiver acceptance and operator reports do not establish chain execution; owner balance recovery must be validated independently against settled history.

Evidence and future routes

The retained November 16 Solana Devnet checkpoint verifies two finalized private credits and complete aggregate accounting. This source description does not repeat those operations or establish today's deployed state. Confidential spending, mixed-action accounting, optional identity attestations and Base, Arbitrum or Polygon PoS integration remain separate future work.
