# Sender-authorized transfer with an offline recipient

**Status: EXPERIMENTAL first-release v1 offline implementation with scoped local verification, 2026-11-23.** The selected transfer contract requires the sender's approval and balance opening, while the recipient remains offline. It replaces the earlier [two-opening core](EXP-20261123-private-transfer-core.md) and [participating-owner recovery](EXP-20261123-transfer-owner-recovery.md) for operational design; their dated results remain historical evidence. Existing wallet deposit/credit behavior is unchanged.

## Sender-only preparation and atomic point credit

[`prepare_transfer`](../../../tsn-protocol/programs/gpru/circuits/hidden_head_transfer_v1/offline/core.py) authenticates both routes and predecessor heads against independently supplied root-selected TIP/TCAP snapshots. The sender supplies a balance/blinding opening and the existing Ed25519 approval bound to both routes, registered asset, private amount commitment, deployment, nonce and validity interval. Sender old/new balances fit u128 and satisfy `oldBalance = newBalance + amount`; the transfer amount remains a positive u64 in registered-asset base units. This admits legitimately accumulated deposit credits above u64 without changing existing deposit instructions or credit relations.

The recipient's prior balance point is selected from the authenticated portfolio. Its successor is `priorPoint + amount * BASE8 + blindingDelta * H`, the same homomorphic credit operation used by the existing deposit-credit path. The circuit authenticates the predecessor point, selected asset membership and exact increment; the recipient supplies no numeric balance, blinding opening, seed, signature or online approval. A missing recipient asset starts from the identity point with an authenticated empty asset path. The recipient head path is checked after the sender update, so one final root covers both legs and one stable sender nonce-derived nullifier.

[`prepare_sender_transfer`](../../../tsn-protocol/programs/gpru/circuits/hidden_head_transfer_v1/offline/sender.py) accepts one sender disclosure packet and the independently validated sender registry/owner pin. `withTinOwnerTransferOpeningV1` uses the existing wallet-owner authentication and supported finalized credit-history recovery to obtain the selected sender opening locally; `signTinTransferOpeningV1` signs its separate disclosure consent. Both helpers require leg `0`, and recipient disclosure packets are rejected. The packet's canonical `TSN_TIN_TRANSFER_OPENING_V1\0` message binds the selected roots/global sequence, sender route key, authorization, asset, predecessor sequence/Memo, u128 balance and canonical blinding. No owner seed or reusable seed-access signature is disclosed to the preparer.

The adapter checks sender disclosure and payment approval independently, validates both registered record public keys and generates the two encrypted successor records. It pins the same private blinding deltas when rebuilding the final witness with actual Memo hashes. Provisional state stays in memory; the caller must independently validate the selected state provenance and verify the exact proof before treating a candidate as accepted. This same-asset, fee-free paired transfer has zero custody/reserve-category deltas. Deposit funding, individual deposit credit and the separate credit-only epoch accounting runtime retain their existing contracts.

## Balance bound and provenance assumptions

There is no local numeric recipient range proof in this transfer circuit. Sender and later owner-recovery arithmetic accept u128, while admitted positive credit amounts remain u64. The lifetime bound relies on authenticated canonical root lineage: with nonresetting u64 sequences and at most a u64 credit amount per admitted action, accumulated positive credits remain below `2^128`, far below the BabyJub subgroup order. Valid initialization, admitted credit relations, complete lineage and absence of arbitrary root resets are premises of that argument; a supplied point or a snapshot matching a caller-provided root does not prove those premises.

This is a source-level design assumption requiring program/root-lineage review, rather than an audited guarantee or a new deposit-credit range check. New transfer sender openings are bounded directly by the circuit, and SDK recovery reconstructs the resulting point, but neither independently establishes the entire network's historical state provenance. Live integration must authenticate canonical state and preserve sequence/replay enforcement before using this relation.

## Encrypted records and later owner recovery

The public statement retains twenty scalars in the [source README's order](../../../tsn-protocol/programs/gpru/circuits/hidden_head_transfer_v1/README.md#atomic-statement-and-abi). Each leg's complete `TSN_GPRU_TRANSFER_HEAD_V1` Memo is SHA-256-reduced into its successor-head commitment. Records reuse the current Ristretto ephemeral agreement, DBrix Secretbox envelope and BabyJub scan-key delta cipher under the registered public keys; no permanent Node decryption key or recipient decryption is introduced. The strict encrypted payload remains `tsn-gpru-transfer-action-v1` with action kind `4` and explicit debit/credit leg.

The circuit binds Memo hashes but does not prove correct encryption or decryptability. `recoverGpruTransferOwnerOpeningV1` therefore requires the exact independently verified twenty-input statement and an authenticated predecessor opening. It checks the Memo hash, leg, successor fields, amount/action commitments and predecessor chain, decrypts the delta and reconstructs the updated Pedersen point with u128 arithmetic. The recipient performs this recovery later when available; its private recovery inputs never become transfer-preparation requirements. The helper does not verify Groth16, a finalized receipt or complete live transfer history itself.
