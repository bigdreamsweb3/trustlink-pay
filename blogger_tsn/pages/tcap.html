TCAP: Confidential Assets, Reserves and Private TIN Credits

Transfer Confidential Asset Protocol governs accepted assets, reserve funding and proof-verified private credit within TSN hybrid infrastructure.

What TCAP owns

TCAP governs asset acceptance, reserve vaults, funded obligations, an authenticated global funding tree, the shared private-state root and asset-specific credit accounting epochs. GPRU verifies the hidden route and balance proof; TSNP enforces settlement authorization. TCAP remains responsible for custody and atomic accounting.

Deposit and individual credit

A wallet-signed deposit transfers the approved SPL token into TCAP's reserve vault and appends a leaf bound to the genuine observed asset and amount. A single confirmed deposit can support a private TIN credit. The Node proves funded membership and the exact private amount, and a secret-derived nullifier prevents reuse of that note.

Successful individual credit advances the shared head/nullifier root and appends a blinded commitment to the asset epoch. It transfers no reserve tokens and reclassifies no public per-credit liability counters. Current private credit does not create a public per-TIN TCAP tip account.

Complete aggregate accounting

A separate proof covers the complete ordered transcript of 2–64 accepted credits. TCAP verifies it through GPRU before moving the authenticated total from pending to settled confidential liabilities. Custody assets, total liabilities and uncredited funding remain unchanged. The batch minimum applies to accounting; it does not make an individual credit wait for another deposit.

Owner history and privacy

The encrypted owner action record travels in Memo instruction data. Owner recovery must validate the recovered commitment openings, authorization, sequence and predecessor relationship against the settled statement. Complete owner recovery remains a separate unverified outcome in the cited November 16 checkpoint. Public wallet funding, aggregate totals, roots, accounts and timing can still reveal information; Node access to private routes and openings is an intentional boundary.

Implementation status

TSN remains an experimental first release. The retained Devnet evidence verifies two private credits and complete aggregate accounting. Confidential debit, TIN-to-TIN conservation, wallet exit, mixed-action accounting and cross-network payouts need their own reviewed proof and runtime evidence.
