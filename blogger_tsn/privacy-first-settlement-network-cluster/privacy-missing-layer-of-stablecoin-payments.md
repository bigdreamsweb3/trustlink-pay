# Why Privacy Is Becoming the Missing Layer of Stablecoin Payments

**Role:** Supporting trend article
**Suggested slug:** `privacy-missing-layer-of-stablecoin-payments`
**Suggested labels:** Stablecoins, Privacy, Payments, Institutional Finance, Settlement, Research, TSN
**Primary intent:** Connect stablecoin growth to the need for private and policy-aware payment infrastructure.

## Required argument

Stablecoins provide programmable value and fast settlement, but public transaction visibility can expose business relationships and personal financial activity. Privacy is one of the adoption layers required for payroll, treasury, merchant, and cross-border workflows.
