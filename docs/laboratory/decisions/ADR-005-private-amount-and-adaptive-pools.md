# Private credit amounts and shared funded roots

**Status: EXPERIMENTAL first-release v1 contract, revised 2026-11-14.** The user retired sealed funding-pool accounts and two-depositor release scheduling. Current credit proves genuine funded value through the existing global funding account while keeping the individual amount private. Separate complete epoch work preserves aggregate accounting. The [ABI](../credit-abi.md) is canonical; the [November 8 record](../experiments/EXP-20261108-remove-funding-pools.md) distinguishes source/offline checks from unperformed activation and live settlement.

## Observed cause and historical boundary

The [November 5 public evidence](../../references/funded-note-public-link-20261105.json) shows 500000 base units in outer/nested credit arguments and a selected tree containing exactly one deposit. That implementation also moved public pending/settled liability counters by each credit amount. Its unblinded action and authorization commitments permitted amount-guessing checks. A circuit-private witness did not conceal values separately serialized or inferable from public account deltas. Those immutable transactions retain their disclosures.

Secret-derived funded-note nullifiers had already removed the earlier public deposit-signature formula. A singleton root still identified the public funding wallet for that credit event; it did not by itself reveal the plaintext recipient TIN or legal identity. [Recipient-registry/head linkage](../experiments/EXP-20261103-native-credit-linkability.md) is a separate historical finding. Alternative deposits can widen source candidates, while identical/known amounts, prior spends and timing can narrow them again.

## Adopted amount and authorization contract

The actual positive u64 amount remains private in exact funded-membership and recipient arithmetic. Fresh nonzero canonical BN254 blinding produces `Poseidon5(TSN_CREDIT_VALUE_V2,tokenId,amount,privateBlinding,nullifier)`; the domain is 1880519585059740412114005250091737410720192050. Action commitment binds action, token, this commitment, reduced authorization and nullifier. TSNP digest binds the commitment, shared root/sequence/slots, token/nullifier, authenticated funding root and epoch account. No plaintext amount is serialized in the current credit call.

The credit remains thirteen public inputs with the funding root last, but its changed circuit requires new matching keys. The TSNP authorization instruction is 905 bytes with sixteen outer accounts, including the readonly reserve and funding-root record plus writable shared root/epoch. The encrypted owner record carries the amount opening; SDK verifies it against the public commitment. Independent historical 825/857-byte readers preserve recovery without an active old serializer or settlement fallback. The full encrypted fixture is 3344/4096 native bytes; arbitrary ciphertext sizes still face the limit.

The selected first-release contract keeps classic-token wallet-to-reserve deposits public and the individual TIN-credit amount private in its proof/commitment. **PROPOSED future integration requirement:** an eligible Token-2022 confidential asset should use its confidential-transfer capability for funding. That route is not implemented or activated by the current classic-token deposit flow.

## Aggregate conservation contract

Deposits remain public SPL transfers and atomically create funded pending obligations. Individual accepted credits consume secret-derived nullifiers, advance the private head/root and append `Poseidon3(previousDigest,amountCommitment,nullifier)` to the asset epoch, without tokens moving or public liabilities being reclassified.

Node reconstructs all successful epoch credits in order and loads the protected opening journal. The separate five-input circuit proves seed, final digest, total, count and token for all 2–64 entries. Missing/reordered/mismatched openings fail; a singleton carries forward. Receiver serializes native verification and holds the lock across an uncertain signed attempt. Cranker submits one Mother-authorized `account_credit_epoch_v1`; TCAP/GPRU authenticate the transcript and total, move only that total from pending to settled confidential liabilities, mark the epoch accounted and advance the reserve epoch. Total liabilities, custody backing and uncredited pending deposits remain unchanged. Replay, insolvency and arithmetic failure reject before mutation.

Aggregate totals remain public and can disclose individual values through sparse/known-amount inference. Existing credited liabilities and historical head/nullifier state are preserved; earlier signature-only uncredited deposits cannot be invented into new-tree members. There is no generic old-pending-obligation migration in this change. Retained Epoch Treasury, AcceptedIntent, Settlement DNA, payout/refund and disabled debit/exit APIs are separate capabilities.

## Funding validation without pool accounts

A confirmed deposit atomically records its asset/amount-bound funded commitment in TCAP. Individual credit proves private membership, amount/token equality and one-time nullifier consumption against current shared spending state. It does not wait for another depositor or create a capture account/transaction.

The existing global funding PDA retains a bounded history of 128 authoritative root/count pairs. Deposits update that history atomically; TCAP/GPRU accept only a current or retained root. Node uses current funding state for preparation and confirms selected-root/count retention before publication/authorization. History tolerates later deposits without reviving historical nullifier state; eviction requires fresh unsigned verification under the existing lease/checkpoint boundaries. The account grows in place to 6,209 bytes through a separate governance-authorized migration that preserves the original root/count/frontier. Its capacity is a concurrency bound, not a latency guarantee.

The credit circuit, private amount commitment, thirteen public inputs and 905-byte TSNP authorization statement remain unchanged. The readonly funding-account position now identifies the canonical global PDA. Receiver/Node/Cranker retain versioned leases, exact verified statement signing, fresh final blockhash selection, durable checkpoints and signed receipt reconciliation. Complete epoch accounting remains considered after 120 slots or 64 accepted credits and requires 2–64 credits; it is separate from individual credit readiness.

The previous two-note/two-wallet pool admission, Mother-attested source census, adaptive release planner and pool-specific recovery are RETIRED. The November 7 experiment and dated logs preserve their historical outcomes. Existing on-chain pool accounts and stored attempts remain historical state until separately authorized cleanup; they are not a runtime fallback. The runtime claims amount confidentiality and valid funded membership, not a guaranteed source anonymity set or timing unlinkability.

## Liquidity providers and limits

The user intends liquidity providers to support ongoing TSN operation and earn transaction fees, not participate only at exits. Provider capital/ownership, earned fee liabilities and user custody obligations require distinct accounting. This implementation does not select admission, fee allocation, accrual, claim, withdrawal or loss policies. The existing liquidity skeleton and disabled exit handler do not establish working provider rewards. Real provider funds count as alternative candidates only when they satisfy the funded-note contract; TSN-controlled addresses do not prove independence.

Node knows private openings and routes. Public source wallets, asset, deposit amounts, epoch totals, roots, successor points, ciphertext, accounts and timing remain observable. Neither encrypted balances nor accepted proofs establish complete privacy, recipient unlinkability, scanner completeness or owner-only knowledge.

## Proposed mixed-action accounting

**Status: mixed-action batching remains PROPOSED, discussed on 2026-11-16; the November 23 offline-recipient transfer below is a separate experiment.** The target is a complete per-asset accounting batch covering accepted confidential financial actions, including credits and debits, with a possible capacity of 64 actions. Current runtime discovery, transcript and aggregate proof cover positive private credits only. Governance, setup and unrelated instruction families do not automatically become financial entries because they call TCAP.

An internal TIN-to-TIN transfer reduces one private balance and increases another by the same amount. With no payout or fee, custody assets and total settled obligations remain unchanged; the proof must enforce both legs atomically. An exit instead binds a private debit to a withdrawal obligation and actual payout. Provider fees, refunds and uncredited funding require their own conserved liability categories. A debit amount cannot simply be subtracted from reserve obligations while ignoring the destination credit or outstanding withdrawal.

The replacement must bind action type, asset, private amount commitment, authorization and replay identity into every ordered transcript entry. Its proof must cover all accepted entries, validate each balance/funding relation and authenticate the resulting category deltas. The current positive-credit sum has no debit sign, transfer pair, fee/refund categories or zero/negative-total contract. Publishing a net or gross batch total can still reveal values through known activity; batching public accounting does not hide an observable wallet payout.

The [November 23 offline-recipient transfer](../experiments/EXP-20261123-offline-recipient-transfer.md) selects one paired action for a same-asset, fee-free transfer: the sender opens and debits its balance, the recipient's authenticated point receives the matching increment, one nullifier is consumed and all reserve-category deltas are zero. The recipient supplies no opening/signature and can remain offline. Its separate twenty-input circuit is EXPERIMENTAL and changes neither existing deposit credit nor the aggregate circuit. Sender/recovered balances use u128, while amounts remain u64; the unopened recipient's lifetime bound depends on canonical authenticated root lineage and nonresetting sequence/admitted-credit limits, not a local numeric recipient range proof or audited guarantee. Two encrypted records permit later owner recovery, which separately checks proof-bound Memo hashes, decryptability and reconstructed successor points. The earlier [two-opening core](../experiments/EXP-20261123-private-transfer-core.md) and [participating-owner extension](../experiments/EXP-20261123-transfer-owner-recovery.md) retain historical test evidence and no longer direct operational design.

Decisions remain open on which actions share a live batch, the per-asset capacity, permitted public totals, empty/singleton maturity, fees, payout finalization and epoch-account reuse. Any account reuse must preserve proof/authorization bindings and prevent old-epoch replay. Inspect the participating program, circuit, SDK, Node, Receiver and Cranker contracts before implementing that replacement. The current 120-slot maturity and 64-credit capacity remain documented in the [credit ABI](../credit-abi.md).
