# Decentralized Settlement Networks: Beyond Blockchain Settlement Layers

**Role:** Supporting taxonomy article
**Suggested slug:** `decentralized-settlement-networks-beyond-blockchain-layers`
**Suggested labels:** Settlement, Payments, Blockchain Infrastructure, Stablecoins, Research, TSN
**Primary intent:** Define the difference between a settlement layer and settlement infrastructure.

## Required argument

A blockchain settlement layer secures and finalizes state. Settlement infrastructure coordinates identity, intent, authorization, liquidity, routing, execution, receipts, and reconciliation around that state. Explain why the terms are related but not interchangeable.
