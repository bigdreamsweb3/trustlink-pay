BULLETIN 02 — THE PROBLEM

Payments need clearer recipient identity.

Wallet payments often require copying a long address. Recipient confirmation, exact approval and settlement evidence remain separate application responsibilities.

Public transfers can reveal counterparties and amounts. Network-specific asset and fee rules complicate payment routing and recovery.

TSN addresses the coordination layer: ten-digit payment identity, exact authorization, private-credit proof verification and reserve accounting.

Lost moneyOne wrong character and funds are gone. Address poisoning steals from wallets every year.
Lost privacySender, recipient and balance metadata sit on-chain for anyone to read.
Lost timeNetwork, fee and transaction-state differences complicate recipient confirmation and recovery.
Missed mainstreamRecipient identity and clear approvals can improve the payment experience.
