TSN separates user authorization, service verification, private-state protection, and settlement authority. The sender's wallet authorizes the requested payment. TSN services verify and route work. GPRU is designed to guard the integrity and privacy of the private state presented to TCAP. TCAP governs accepted assets, liquidity, and atomic settlement.

## Participant boundaries

| Participant | Role | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sender wallet | Reviews and authorizes the payment. | A service or Cranker cannot replace the sender's approval. |
| TSN services | Coordinate and verify payment work. | Service acceptance or verification is not on-chain settlement. |
| Cranker | Submits authorized work and pays transaction fees. | Submission does not prove confirmation or give authority to alter payment terms. |
| GPRU | Guards the user's private state presented to TCAP. | A proof does not hold funds or replace TCAP checks. |
| TCAP | Governs accepted assets and applies authorized settlement. | The transition must satisfy the applicable asset, authorization, and liquidity rules. |

## Identity privacy and public-chain visibility

The native-credit ABI hides the recipient route/head witness and does not encode a plaintext TIN or private predecessor pointer. The owner retains its record lineage. Shared roots, public statement fields, encrypted Memo bytes, accounts, timing and funding wallets remain observable; the design does not establish that metadata or authorized Node/application knowledge cannot correlate payments. Confidential debit/exit remains a separate unverified execution path.

The funded-note source derives the public nullifier from a private nonzero note secret with a domain-separated Poseidon hash. TCAP commits the registered asset and observed transfer amount in an append-only funding leaf; the circuit privately proves its membership and exact credit binding before consuming the nullifier. The credit statement excludes the note commitment, funded leaf/index and opening, removing the old public signature-to-nullifier calculation. Node receives the restricted opening and routing witness, so owner-only knowledge is not established. Public deposits/epoch totals, timing and registry/head correlations remain distinct limits.

Sealed funding capture authenticates the chain-current root with at least two positive unspent member notes and two source wallets. Later deposits cannot alter the captured root. Zero-value actors and same-wallet duplicates cannot increase source count; wallet addresses do not prove independent participants. Credits remain serialized by their evolving private root. Signed uncertain attempts require reconciliation before replacement; unsigned stale work is reverified. New matching credit/epoch keys and programs require operator activation.

## Capability status

A later read-only check of the first finalized native credit reconstructed its successor global root from a public TIP registry's route-key commitment and public credit fields, starting from its verified empty initial maps. This identifies a persistent public recipient registry without decrypting its ten-digit TIN. Offline controls also reproduced later same-registry and cross-asset portfolio correlations under known-history assumptions. Hiding fields from proof inputs does not establish unlinkability when their commitment can be reconstructed from enumerable public values. The [source-backed investigation](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/blob/main/docs/laboratory/experiments/EXP-20261103-native-credit-linkability.md) gives the bounded evidence and remaining checks.

The hidden-head proof flow is not established as an end-to-end settlement capability, and confidential debit and exit are not established as available. A source-level design or accepted service request is not proof of settlement; rely on the confirmed chain transaction and resulting state.

The detailed adversary assumptions and implementation evidence are in the [repository security documentation](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/tree/main/docs).

## Funding membership sets

The secret-derived nullifier removes the old public deposit-signature calculation, but privacy also depends on the eligible deposit set. In the November 5 reference run, the credit's public funding root exactly reconstructs from its sole index-zero deposit, and the public funding account contains one leaf. An observer can therefore link that deposit to the credit without recovering the secret or recipient TIN. Both historical nullifier controls reproduced; the updated nullifier differed from the former formula. See [the public-only evidence and method](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/blob/main/docs/laboratory/experiments/EXP-20261103-funded-note-credit.md#public-only-deposit-to-credit-regression-2026-11-05).

With several deposits, a shared root represents a membership set rather than identifying one consumed leaf. Public token/amount, timing and known prior spends can reduce that set. This investigation establishes a singleton link for the supplied pair; it does not establish multi-deposit unlinkability or retest recipient-registry correlations.

## Private credit amount and aggregate visibility

The experimental private-credit source uses fresh blinded amount commitments and leaves reserve liabilities unchanged on individual credit. Node-created, Receiver-coordinated and Cranker-submitted epoch work proves all 2–64 accepted openings before one aggregate liability reclassification. Actual credit amount is absent from instruction fields and the old unblinded guessing formulas, but public epoch totals and known candidate amounts can still reveal values by inference. Common adaptive windows do not prove timing unlinkability or independent participants. Offline real-proof/native checks passed; SBF, activation, live trials and independent review remain unverified. See [the decision](https://github.com/bigdreamsweb3/trustlink-pay/blob/main/docs/laboratory/decisions/ADR-005-private-amount-and-adaptive-pools.md).
