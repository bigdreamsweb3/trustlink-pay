## Resolved disagreements

8. **Privacy guarantees.** The experimental replacement hides individual credit amounts with blinded commitments and performs liability reclassification in complete grouped epochs. Deposit/aggregate amounts, roots, token, points, ciphertext, timing, known history and Node knowledge remain visible or trusted. Recipient-registry correlation has separate historical evidence. 12. **Historical public deposit-nullifier linkage.** The November 2 native credit derived its published nullifier from public TCAP program/deposit-signature bytes and a fixed domain, allowing observers to recompute the funding-to-credit link. This contradicted that version’s no-public-funding-pointer claims while the TIN witness remained hidden. The current funded-note circuit uses private-secret membership/nullifier binding; [cryptography](cryptography.md) records that replacement and the remaining public metadata/recipient-head correlation boundaries. The historical field-map evidence below remains valid for its original transaction.

13. **Registry versus global-state version.** Earlier current guides called fresh TIN registration schema 3. TIP `state.rs` uses `CURRENT_VERSION=3` for global state and `TinV1Account::VERSION=1` for the current registry; staging is version 4. The [successful run](experiments/EXP-20261102-tin-staged-finalization.md) independently found registry version 1. Current guides now use the actual registry/layout contract. Older readiness descriptions retain their original schema labels with an explicit correction, and an account version byte alone does not prove old-layout compatibility.
14. **Cranker fee payer and confirmation scope.** The public operator guide implied Cranker pays every submitted Solana fee. Current funding wire fixes the SDK depositor as payer; Cranker pays its constructed TIN/native-credit transactions. The [transaction map](cranker-transaction-map.md) also distinguishes terminal daemon reports from Receiver's available SUBMITTED state, and Creditcoin hub confirmation from destination payout.

## Remaining claim review

Whole-page CURRENT means a reviewed source narrative, not a tested deployment. VERIFIED labels apply to specified observations with linked evidence; environment observations retain their captured date/slot. Legacy runbooks, research assertions, generalized cross-chain execution, threshold-provider activation, historical selection rationale and many generated JSDoc assertions remain UNVERIFIED where the supporting evidence is insufficient. Generated TypeScript signatures describe source contracts but do not make every associated comment true.

The claim inventory deliberately includes code/diagram units without copying their bodies; complete examples, source extraction and syntax reports supply different evidence. A prose paragraph can contain several assertions, so a future detailed review should split a mixed paragraph into individually scoped claims and record evidence per claim. The register does not claim that an automated paragraph extraction proved every sentence. Re-run inventory after edits so positions/hashes remain useful.

## Finalized-credit privacy claim boundary

**Later checkpoint, 2026-11-03:** [public registry/root reconstruction](experiments/EXP-20261103-native-credit-linkability.md) linked the actual first credit to its persistent TIP registry using public data and verified empty initial maps. The TIN witness's absence from proof inputs does not establish recipient unlinkability: its head key is public registry material, and the first successor head is reconstructible. Fourteen offline structural controls also reproduced later same-registry and cross-asset portfolio correlations. This is separate from the earlier funding-nullifier calculation and does not recover the plaintext TIN or validate a second live credit.

The [first-credit field map](experiments/EXP-20261102-native-credit-baseline.md) independently recomputed the existing public nullifier from its funding signature and read the public 100000-base-unit amount. This contradicts full funding-to-credit unlinkability and amount confidentiality for that transaction while preserving the narrower accepted hidden-head/encrypted-owner-record milestone. The formula and amount argument were already present in the saved Git version; confirmation/receipt repairs did not add the disclosure. Funding-wallet correlation alone does not identify the recipient TIN, and a future comparison must inspect transformed relationships as well as literal field reuse.
