# Why Public Blockchain Payments Cannot Be Private by Default

**Role:** Supporting article
**Suggested slug:** `why-public-blockchain-payments-cannot-be-private-by-default`
**Suggested labels:** Payments, Privacy, Stablecoins, Blockchain, Research, TSN
**Primary intent:** Explain the privacy limits of transparent ledgers without implying that privacy requires anonymity or illegal activity.

## Required argument

Public ledgers optimize for shared verification. Payment privacy requires limiting exposure of amounts, counterparties, balances, and financial relationships. Explain the difference between pseudonymity, confidentiality, selective disclosure, and anonymity.
