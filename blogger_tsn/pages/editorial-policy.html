Editorial Policy

This publication aims to make technical payment infrastructure understandable while keeping research claims, implementation status, and external evidence distinct.

Scope

Articles cover Transfer Settlement Network architecture, blockchain payments, identity-aware routing, privacy, settlement, and related ecosystem research. The publication is educational research and protocol documentation, not financial, legal, or regulatory advice.

Evidence and attribution

Definitions, comparisons, and claims about external products or protocols should link to primary institutional, technical, or academic sources where practical. TrustLink Labs separates documented external behavior from its own proposed architecture and implementation notes.

Accuracy and status

Technical status is stated as precisely as possible. Planned, experimental, devnet, and production claims are not interchangeable. Readers should verify current implementation details in the linked repositories and specifications.

Independence and disclosures

Advertising, sponsorship, or an external link does not determine editorial conclusions. See the Advertising and Cookie Disclosure for site disclosures.

Corrections

Report a factual error with the page URL, relevant passage, and supporting evidence through the Contact page. Corrections are reviewed and documented according to the Corrections Policy.
