TrustLink Labs is building foundational infrastructure for the **Internet of Value** through **TSN**, a hybrid settlement network designed to make value transfer interoperable, identity-aware, and privacy-preserving across applications and blockchain networks. TSN combines off-chain coordination and verification with on-chain authorization and asset settlement. Cross-network interoperability is an architectural goal; individual routes must be verified before they are described as live.

The network combines protocol layers with application SDKs so a payment can be addressed through a portable identity, authorized by a wallet, checked against private state, and settled under asset-specific rules.

**TSN** names the full infrastructure. **TSNP** is the on-chain Transfer Settlement Network Protocol. **TIP** manages transfer identity, including the 10-digit **TIN**. **GPRU** guards the integrity and privacy of the user's private state presented to **TCAP**. TCAP governs the accepted assets, reserves, and settlement transition. **DIP** describes the deposit-ingress protocol for external value. Circle's **CCTP** is a separate protocol used only where an integration supports it.

The design goal is to keep identity, private-state verification, and asset governance in distinct roles. GPRU's proof is intended to show that a hidden state is current and that a proposed change follows from it; TCAP remains the authority over funds and applies authorized changes atomically.

The hidden-head proof path and confidential debit/exit are not established as end-to-end capabilities.
