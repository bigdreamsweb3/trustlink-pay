About the Transfer Settlement Network

Transfer Settlement Network (TSN) is the hybrid decentralized blockchain settlement protocol infrastructure we are building for the Internet of Value. A ten-digit TIN gives people and businesses a recognizable blockchain payment identity, while owner-consented attested credentials help senders confirm the intended recipient. Users share an identity for payments rather than treating a public wallet address as their everyday identifier.

About TrustLink Labs

TrustLink Labs is a research and protocol-development initiative led by Agbaka Matthew Daniel, also known as Big Dreams Web3. The work explores how blockchain systems can coordinate payment identity, authorization, settlement execution, confidentiality, and final ownership updates.

What this publication covers

This publication explains the TSN architecture and related components, including TIN payment identity, GPRU proof verification, the TSN Node, Crankers, TSNP authorization and TCAP confidential asset accounting. Articles distinguish documented implementation evidence from proposed architecture and future work.

The product vision

People should be able to fund a payment identity, send stablecoin value, confirm the recipient and follow a clear settlement receipt. Developers can build wallets and payment experiences on one identity and authorization model, with familiar payment interfaces above chain-specific settlement. Owner-consented, issuer-verified personal and business credentials are part of the intended recipient-assurance experience, with revocation and selective disclosure rather than a display name treated as proof.

Solana provides the settlement foundation. Base, Arbitrum and Polygon PoS are the lower-fee EVM integration priorities; Ethereum Layer 1 is outside the present focus. Each network retains its own assets, fees, finality and liquidity rules, which TSN must handle through a supported route.

Development and evidence

The first-release implementation is under development. Retained Solana Devnet evidence covers private wallet-funded TIN credit and complete aggregate accounting; the complete identity and settlement product, EVM delivery and credential-provider integration require further work. Production readiness and privacy assurance are assessed separately from this product vision.
