A Transfer Identity Number (TIN) is the ten-digit recipient identity managed by TIP. It allows an application to resolve recipient information before the sender approves a payment. TSN's Internet of Value direction includes optional issuer-backed credentials that could make a resolved identity more informative, while preserving the distinction between payment authorization and identity verification.

## Current identity behavior

The current SDK and Node resolve an owner-provided display name from the encrypted TIN identity envelope. That name is a confirmation aid, not proof of a government-verified person or registered business. The payment message binds the resolved name and credential list, and Node checks them against its own resolution. Current resolution returns an empty verified-credential list.

The SDK can serialize credential-shaped metadata, including a type, issuer and claims, into an authorization message. Serialization and matching do not authenticate an external issuer. The inspected TIP account and resolver do not establish an integration with Solana Attestation Service, Sumsub or another identity-verification provider. See [TIP and TIN](/layers/tin) for current registry and recovery behavior.

## Proposed optional attestations

An attestation extension could let a TIN owner attach independently verified personal or business credentials and choose which supported claims a sender may see. Examples include a verified name or business registration claim. Integration with Solana or another network's attestation system would require an adopted credential schema, trusted issuer rules, proof of the binding to the TIN owner, expiry and revocation checks, and consent-aware disclosure.

An issuer statement about a name and a wallet signature establish different facts. Wallet control alone does not prove legal identity; a verified name alone does not authorize a payment. Applications would need to explain the issuer, claim scope and validity without exposing sensitive documents or promising that every credential is accepted across networks.

This extension remains **PROPOSED**. No provider partnership, deployed attestation route or mandatory identity-verification requirement is announced by this page.
